Choosing your seats

It's late June. You're booking August flights from Madrid to Rome with your partner and child. You're in the middle of the booking process and now need to choose your seats.

What to do

Rank the options from the one you'd mind least to the one you'd mind most. Then use the two lines to show your boundaries.

Please think aloud as you go. Say whatever comes to mind while you move things around, even if it feels obvious or unfinished. How you get there matters more to us than where you end up.
